# EP00: Meet Coconut! (channel intro, ~70 sec)

**Goal:** Introduce Coconut and the idea of the channel: she is cuddly with her family and a little shy with new people, and that is okay.
**Age:** 2-5. **Language:** English. **Voice:** Narrator (you, warm and slow). Coconut only says short sounds.
**Style:** Calm pace, short sentences, repeated phrases. No scratching, hissing or loud scares.

## Intro song (4 lines, 0:00-0:08, sing or read slowly)
> 🎵 *Coconut, Coconut, soft as a cloud,*
> *Shy with strangers, but sweet and proud!*
> *With Mom and Dad she snuggles tight,*
> *Coconut, Coconut, goodnight, goodnight!* 🎵

## Scene 1 (0:00-0:08): Hello!
**Visual:** Coconut sits in her room, serious look, blinks slowly. Tail sways. Caption: "Meet Coconut!"
**Narrator:** "Hello, friends! This is Coconut."

## Scene 2 (0:08-0:18): Who is Coconut?
**Visual:** Coconut tilts her head. Ear twitches.
**Narrator:** "Coconut is a soft, fluffy, gray cat. She has big green eyes... and a little flower collar with a bell!"
**Coconut (bubble, 0:10):** "Hmph."
**Caption:** "Soft, fluffy, and gray"

## Scene 3 (0:18-0:30): Mom and Dad
**Visual:** Coconut relaxes, eyes soften, tail wags faster.
**Narrator:** "Coconut loves her Mom and Dad. She snuggles with them, and she sleeps with them every night."
**Coconut (bubble, 0:24):** "Purr... purr..."
**Caption:** "She loves Mom and Dad"

## Scene 4 (0:30-0:43): A visitor!
**Visual:** Doorbell. Coconut's eyes go wide, ears back. She slides down and hides behind her tunnel, then peeks out.
**Bubble (0:31):** "Ding dong!"
**Narrator:** "But when a visitor comes... Coconut is a little shy."
**Coconut (bubble, 0:38):** "Hmm... who is it?"
**Caption:** "But she is shy with visitors"

## Scene 5 (0:43-0:58): It is okay to be shy
**Visual:** Coconut slowly comes out, eyes a little softer.
**Narrator:** "That is okay! Shy friends need a little time. And then... Coconut says hello."
**Coconut (bubble, 0:43):** "Hello... a little."
**Caption:** "Shy friends need a little time"

## Scene 6 (0:58-1:10): See you soon
**Visual:** Coconut sits calmly, tail sways.
**Narrator:** "Come and play with Coconut every week! See you next time. Bye-bye!"
**Coconut (bubble, 1:06):** "Bye... bye!"
**Caption:** "See you next time!"

## Recording notes (for the voice recording)
- Speak slowly, smile while you talk, pause one second between sentences.
- Record in a quiet room as a **WAV** file. Then run Rhubarb to get mouth data (see README).
- Coconut's sounds ("Hmph.", "Purr...", "Hmm...", "Hello... a little.", "Bye... bye!") can be recorded in a soft, tiny voice or added as sound effects.

## Instagram match
Short real video of Coconut's serious stare, her flower collar, and (if safe) her peeking from the tunnel. Caption: "This is Coconut. She is shy with visitors but she always warms up. 🐱" Keep addresses, plates and documents out of the frame.

## Character checklist
- [x] Gray fur, white chest, white paw tips
- [x] Cream flower collar, bell, yellow flower tag
- [x] Green-yellow eyes, serious look as default
- [x] No scratching, hissing or hitting
- [x] No address, plate or personal documents
