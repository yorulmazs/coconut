# EP01: Coconut and the Big Box (~2:40)

**Lesson (target words):** position words **IN, ON, BEHIND** (bonus: **OUT**).
**Why this lesson:** "In" and "on" are among the first spatial words children learn (about 2-3 years), "behind" comes a little later (about 2.5-3 years), so the set fits 2-5 year olds: the youngest learn "in/on", older ones get "behind". A box shows all three without any other character. A cat in a box is real cat behaviour, so the Instagram match is easy to film.
**Why not the visitor story now:** EP00 already used the "shy with visitors" arc. Our rule says that arc appears at most once in every 4 episodes (`docs/POLICY_NOTES.md` item 2), so EP01 is a different situation with a different ending.
**Format:** Coconut does not talk. The narrator (Kokoro `af_heart`, speed 0.9) tells the story. Coconut acts with movement, faces, real cat sounds and thought symbols ("!", "?", "...", hearts).
**Teaching technique (research):** each target word is said at least 6 times; the narrator makes a short **dramatic pause before the word** ("Coconut is... IN the box!"), which helped children remember new words better than a pause after the word or no pause; the narrator asks the viewer ("Can you say 'in'?") and waits about 2 seconds. Sources in `docs/RESEARCH.md`.
**Safety:** no scratching, hissing or hitting. Box landings are soft, no loud bangs, no flashing.

## SCENE 1 (0:08-0:22, 14 s): A big box!
**Place:** Coconut's room. A red ball and a toy mouse lie in a corner.
**Visual:**
- A big brown box slides in from the right and stops with a soft wobble (box squash and settle).
- Coconut: eyes wide, ears up, small hop back (anticipation down, then up). Symbol **"!"**.
- Then curiosity: head tilt, look at the box, ears forward, one ear twitches, slow lean forward to sniff (body stretch forward, nose toward box). Symbol **"?"**.
**Narrator:**
"Oh! What is this?"
*(pause)*
"It's a box. A big... brown... box!"
"Coconut is curious. Sniff, sniff."
**Sound:** soft cardboard slide, gentle bump. Coconut: short curious "mrrp?" (real cat sound).
**Caption:** "A big box!"

---

## SCENE 2 (0:22-0:48, 26 s): IN
**Visual:**
- Coconut crouches (squash down, tail low), then jumps up and drops into the box (stretch in the air, squash on landing).
- Box wobbles. For a moment we see nothing. Then the ears pop up, then the eyes peek over the edge. Eyes look left, look right.
- Bell on the collar swings after the landing.
**Narrator:**
"Hop! Where is Coconut?"
*(pause 1.5 s, ears appear)*
"Coconut is... **IN** the box!"
"In. In the box."
"Can you say 'in'?"
*(pause 2 s, Coconut's ears twitch as if listening)*
"In! Great job!"
**Visual (out):** Coconut springs up and out of the box, lands in front of it with a squash, tail flicks.
**Narrator:**
"And now... Coconut is **OUT**! Out of the box."
**Caption:** "IN" (big, with a small arrow pointing into the box), then "OUT"
**Sound:** soft thump inside the box, cardboard rustle. Coconut: quiet happy chirp.

---

## SCENE 3 (0:48-1:12, 24 s): ON
**Visual:**
- Coconut looks up at the top of the box (head up, eyes up), wiggles her bottom (anticipation), jumps up.
- She lands on top. The box squashes a little, Coconut wobbles, finds her balance, then sits tall and proud: chin up, eyes half closed, tail wraps around her paws. Tail tip flicks.
**Narrator:**
"Up, up... Coconut is... **ON** the box!"
"On. On the box."
"She sits on the box like a little queen."
"Can you say 'on'?"
*(pause 2 s)*
"On! Well done!"
**Sound:** soft pat of paws on cardboard. Coconut: proud little "Hmph." (cat huff sound, not a word).
**Caption:** "ON"

---

## SCENE 4 (1:12-1:36, 24 s): BEHIND
**Visual:**
- Coconut hops down on the far side. Now the box hides her body. Only her **tail** sticks up above the box and waves slowly.
- Camera pushes in a little toward the tail.
- Then her ears and eyes slowly rise over the box edge, she peeks, ducks down again (playful peekaboo, not scared).
**Narrator:**
"Hop! Oh no... where did Coconut go?"
*(pause)*
"I can see her tail! Wiggle, wiggle."
"Coconut is... **BEHIND** the box!"
"Behind. Behind the box."
"Can you say 'behind'?"
*(pause 2 s)*
"Behind! Hooray!"
**Sound:** soft rustle. Peek moment: tiny bell jingle.
**Caption:** "BEHIND"
**Note:** This is a playful hide (peekaboo), not the shy hiding from EP00. Ears stay up, eyes bright, tail relaxed.

---

## SCENE 5 (1:36-2:06, 30 s): Let's play! Where is Coconut?
**Visual:** Three quick rounds. In each round the screen holds Coconut's new spot during the pause so the child can answer, but she is never frozen (breathing, blinking, ear twitch, tail sway).
1. Coconut jumps **on** the box.
2. Coconut slides down **behind** the box (only tail visible).
3. Coconut dives **in** the box (ears peek out).
**Narrator:**
"Let's play a game! Where is Coconut?"
*(round 1, jump)* "Where is she?" *(pause 2 s)* "**On** the box!"
*(round 2, slide)* "Where is she now?" *(pause 2 s)* "**Behind** the box!"
*(round 3, dive)* "And now?" *(pause 2 s)* "**In** the box!"
"You found her every time!"
**Visual (end):** Coconut pops her head up, happy squint, small floating hearts.
**Caption:** "Where is Coconut?" then "ON", "BEHIND", "IN" after each answer (caption appears only after the pause, so the child answers first).
**Sound:** a soft different "boing" for each jump.

---

## SCENE 6 (2:06-2:28, 22 s): The best toy
**Visual:**
- Camera pans to the corner: red ball, toy mouse. Coconut looks at the ball... looks at the mouse... looks at the box. Ears perk.
- She goes into the box, turns around once, settles down. Big yawn (mouth open), eyes slowly close. Purr: body breathes slow and deep, collar rises and falls with the breath.
**Narrator:**
"Coconut has a ball."
"Coconut has a mouse."
"But Coconut likes... the **box** best!"
*(pause)*
"Shh... Coconut is in the box. Time for a cozy nap."
**Sound:** real Coconut purr (if the user records one, otherwise a soft purr effect). Music softens.
**Symbol:** "..." then "z z z" (if added to the symbol set).

---

## SCENE 7 (2:28-2:40, 12 s): Bye-bye
**Visual:** Coconut opens one eye, slow blink at the viewer (cat "I love you" blink), tail tip comes up over the box and waves like a hand.
**Narrator:**
"In... on... behind! Great job, friends!"
"See you next time with Coconut. Bye-bye!"
**Caption:** "In · On · Behind" then "See you next time!"
**Sound:** quiet happy chirp. End music.
